Delete your Locked In account

Locked In by Locked In Labs.

In the app

  1. Open Locked In and tap your profile picture (top right), then Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm with your Google account.
  3. Optional: tick Also erase everything on this phone.

Your cloud backup (workouts, plans, profile, body weight) and your Locked In sign-in are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@lockedinlabs.work from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and all backed-up data within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What's deleted and what's kept

  • Deleted: your account, and every workout, plan, profile detail and body weight entry in your backup.
  • Kept: nothing. We keep no copies after deletion.
  • On your phone: data stored on the device stays until you erase it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
